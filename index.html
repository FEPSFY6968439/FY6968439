Balancing Drag and Downforce in Motorsport

Abstract

This article explores the two most fundamental forces in the world of motorsport which are Downforce and Drag whilst also outlining why balancing these two forces is the most important thing in the world of racing. It explains how downforce is created due to the car’s wings and underside, making the tires have more grip while drag acts in the opposite direction to the motion of travel reducing acceleration and top speed. Furthermore, the article examines how both forces increase with speed and what the top and lead engineers can use to find a suitable balance. By considering the layouts of each individual track they can determine the best possible set up enhancing the speed of the car leading to the fastest lap times.
